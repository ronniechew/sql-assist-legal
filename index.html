SQL Assist — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

SQL Assist ("we", "us") provides WhatsApp-based technical support for SQL Accounting software. This page explains what we collect when you message our WhatsApp number, and how it is used and deleted.

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and the profile name WhatsApp shares with businesses.
  • The content of the messages you send us: text, and voice notes (which are transcribed to text).
  • Basic technical details needed to answer you, such as the error message you report.

How it is used

  • To understand your problem and reply with troubleshooting steps.
  • To hand your conversation to a human technician when the issue cannot be resolved in chat.
  • To keep a record of what was advised, for follow-up and quality review.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who processes it

  • Meta Platforms — WhatsApp Cloud API delivers your messages to us.
  • CommandCode / DeepSeek — an AI model drafts the reply from your message text.
  • Groq — converts voice notes to text.
  • Telegram — notifies our technician when a human is needed.

How long we keep it

Conversation records are kept for up to 30 days for follow-up and quality purposes, then deleted. Escalated conversations are closed automatically after 72 hours without activity.

Your choices

  • You can ask us to delete your conversation data at any time.
  • You can stop using the service simply by not messaging us again.

Data deletion

To request deletion, email flovv.studio@gmail.com with the WhatsApp number you used and the word "delete". We will remove your conversation records within 7 days and confirm by reply.

Contact

Questions about this policy: flovv.studio@gmail.com
